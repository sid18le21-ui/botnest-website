How BotNest handles information shared through this website.

If you contact BotNest by email or use the enquiry form on this website, information you choose to provide may include your name, email address, project details and other contact information.

Information is used to respond to enquiries, discuss requested products or services, prepare quotations and provide customer support.

This website is a static information and enquiry website. The enquiry form creates an email draft in your email application; it does not transmit or store the form contents on this website.

BotNest does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only where needed to operate communications or deliver requested services. Information is retained only as reasonably required for the enquiry or business relationship.
