Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information provided through BotNest Academy's website and registration system is handled.

Information we collect

When an account or course registration is created, the system may collect details such as name, email address, phone number and student/course information needed to provide the service.

How information is used

Information is used to create and manage accounts, process course registrations, communicate about enquiries or registrations, maintain attendance and progress records, and provide the requested academy services.

Account security

Account passwords are processed using server-side password hashing. Session information is used to keep authenticated areas protected.

Data sharing

BotNest Academy does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, authentication and data systems.

Your choices

For questions about personal information associated with an account, contact botnest.officials@gmail.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the website, services or applicable requirements change.
