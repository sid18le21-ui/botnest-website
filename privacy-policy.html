Privacy policy.

This page describes how information shared through the BotNest website may be used.

Information you provide

If you contact BotNest by email, WhatsApp or an enquiry form, you may provide your name, contact details and project information. The website enquiry form currently prepares an email in your own email application; it does not transmit form data to a BotNest server.

How information is used

Information you send may be used to respond to enquiries, understand project requirements, prepare quotations and provide requested support.

Third-party services

Links to email, WhatsApp and other third-party services are governed by the privacy terms of those providers.

Data protection

Please do not send passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information through general enquiry channels.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact botnest.officials@gmail.com.
