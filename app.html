HOW IT WORKS

From a tap to a confirmed action.

A secure application flow connects the user, backend service, message broker and physical controller.

01

Account setup

BotNest provisions the customer account, assigns a BotNest User ID and temporary password, and configures the home's rooms and devices.

02

First-time login

The customer signs in with their BotNest User ID. The app requires a password change before opening the home dashboard.

03

Choose a room

The dashboard shows assigned rooms and connected devices. Open a room to control an appliance individually or use room-level controls.

04

Device response

The app sends an authenticated request to the backend. Commands travel through MQTT to the controller, and the device reports its resulting state.

The application distinguishes a sent command from a confirmed physical device state. If a device is offline or does not acknowledge a command, its status should remain pending, unconfirmed or failed rather than showing a false success.

CUSTOMER FEATURES

Everything organised around your space.

⌂

Home dashboard

See the rooms assigned to your home and access key controls from one overview.

▦

Rooms & devices

Open a room to control individual appliances. Use room-level ON/OFF to send commands to devices assigned to that room.

◷

Routines & schedules

Create time-based actions for selected rooms and appliances. Configure one-time or selected-day schedules, edit them, disable them or remove them.

⌁

Device status

View reported device status and understand when a command is pending, unconfirmed or unsuccessful.

◉

Energy meter & bill estimate

For supported energy-meter installations, the app is planned to present energy-consumption information and an estimated Tamil Nadu electricity bill.

⚙

Settings & support

Manage account details, password, appearance preferences, notifications, privacy information and customer support access.

Energy-meter presentation is intentionally focused on consumption and bill estimation; the customer app is not intended to display measured line voltage, household current or real power values.

ACCESS & PERMISSIONS

The right access for each role.

BOTNEST OPERATIONS

Super Admin

  • Create and manage customer accounts
  • Generate customer IDs and temporary credentials
  • Configure rooms and register devices
  • Assign devices and hardware identities
CUSTOMER ACCOUNT OWNER

Customer Owner

  • Control assigned home devices
  • Rename existing rooms
  • Create and manage routines
  • Manage permitted household access
SHARED HOME ACCESS

Household Member

  • Access permitted rooms and devices
  • Control assigned appliances
  • Use permitted routines
  • Operate within owner-granted permissions

Customer users cannot create or delete rooms, or register and delete devices. Permissions are intended to be enforced by the backend, not only by hiding controls in the app.
