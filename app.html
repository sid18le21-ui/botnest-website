HOW IT WORKS

One connected control path.

The app communicates with the BotNest backend, which validates access and routes commands through the existing MQTT infrastructure to the assigned controller.

01 / USER APPFlutter appHome · Rooms · Routines
→
02 / APPLICATIONFastAPI serviceAuthentication · Permissions · APIs
→
03 / MESSAGINGMQTT brokerCommand delivery · State updates
→
04 / HARDWAREESP32 controllerRelays · Sensors · Actuators

A sent command is not treated as proof that an appliance changed state. The system distinguishes command delivery, acknowledgement and confirmed physical device state.

APP FEATURES

Control, organize and automate.

The app experience is structured around the customer's home, assigned rooms and connected devices.

⌂

Home dashboard

See assigned rooms and connected devices from a central overview, with room-level controls for supported appliances.

▦

Rooms & devices

Open a room to control appliances individually, review their reported state and rename existing rooms where permitted.

◷

Scheduled routines

Create time-based routines, choose assigned devices and actions, and let the backend scheduler execute the routine.

⌘

Role-based access

Super Admin provisions customers and devices. Customer Owners manage household access and routines. Household Members use permitted controls.

◉

Device-state feedback

Controller acknowledgements and reported states are used to update the app. Offline or unconfirmed actions remain pending or failed.

⚙

Settings & support

Access account settings, notification preferences, app information and customer support options.

ACCOUNT & SECURITY MODEL

Access that follows responsibility.

The backend is the authority for identity, permissions and device operations. Customer accounts and device assignments are provisioned by the Super Admin; household users receive access based on their assigned role.

Super Admin

Creates customer accounts, configures homes, rooms, registered devices and hardware/MQTT settings.

Customer Owner

Controls assigned devices, renames rooms, manages routines and household access.

Household Member

Uses shared home devices and permitted routines within assigned permissions.
