08 · Questions

Good questions

Do I have to replace my switches or wiring?

No. A Botnest board is mounted right next to your switchboard, and each appliance gets its own channel. Your switchboard stays.

What is projected and non-projected?

Projected boards are mounted on the wall. Non-projected boards are set into the wall, flush with the surface. We suggest one during the home visit.

What can I control?

Lights, fans, AC, sockets, curtains, water motors and more. We are rolling out 200+ products in phases, starting with switches, lighting, plugs, fans and AC.

Can my family use it?

Yes. Everyone gets their own access, and you decide which rooms and appliances each person can control.

What happens after installation?

One month later we come back and check every board: ventilation, connections, power on every channel, and the app. It’s free.

What if a light or appliance stops working?

Botnest reads the power on each channel. If something is switched on but draws no power, you get an alert, so you know it’s the bulb or the device.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your rooms and the number of channels. Book a free home visit and we’ll give you a clear quote.

Do you work with offices?

Yes. Meeting rooms, floor-wide schedules and energy monitoring, across one or many sites.
